Evaluation Study Design

Same start. Same destination. Three routes.

Within-subjects  ·  3 conditions  ·  counterbalanced  ·  n ≈ 15

Report

Where you are now, and where you want to get to.

Listen

Ten minutes. One condition per session.

Rate

The experience, and how you feel now.

Repeat

All three, then a short interview.

Fixedcontrol

What we measure

  • Did it feel like it was going somewhere?
  • Pleasant?
  • Did it hang together?
  • Which did you prefer?
  • Describe it in your own words
  • Calmness, before and after

Exploratory — we don't expect to shift mood at this scale.

Every condition ends at the same soundscape and runs the same ten minutes. Only the route changes.

What to say

Six lines. One per beat.

  1. Everyone hears all three conditions, in a counterbalanced order — so each person is their own control.
  2. They tell us where they are and where they'd like to get to, then listen for ten minutes.
  3. All three end at the same soundscape and last the same time. The only difference is the path taken to get there.
  4. Afterwards they rate the experience and describe it in their own words.
  5. We're not betting on measuring a mood change — at fifteen people that's not realistic. We're asking whether people notice the journey, and whether they prefer it.
  6. If nobody can tell a guided transition from a fixed soundscape, that's a real result, and we'd find it out cheaply.
